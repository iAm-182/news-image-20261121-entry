A newspaper front page summing up Saturday 21 November 2026 in a single
image, drawn with PIL primitives (original vector artwork, no photos, no
downloaded images). 16 stories, every one first reported on 21 November
2026: the Russian missiles that came down in south-east Poland and the
massive overnight strike on Kyiv; Denmark expelling a Russian diplomat
over an alleged plot to kill rapper Kyivstoner; the US stock plunge into
correction (Nasdaq −5.3%, Nvidia −14%); the UK–EU food and trade deals;
the Trump–Xi–Putin trilateral summit ending with few results; plus
Anthropic's post-IPO fall, GTA VI's $3.79bn first day, Cyclone Akvan,
Estonia shutting its Russian border crossings, the Palisades Fire arson
conviction, the Saudi interception of Houthi missiles over Riyadh, the
Las Vegas Grand Prix under the lights, and US approval of ~880,000 AI
chip sales to China.

Running `draw.py` regenerates `entry.png` (1024×1024, ~145 KB);
`main.py` copies that file to the grader's output folder. Deterministic:
two runs write identical bytes. The footer states the date discipline —
every story on the page was first reported on Saturday 21 November 2026.
